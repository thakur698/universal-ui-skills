# Benchmark 04: Ecommerce Storefront

**STATUS: COMPLETED**

## Task
"Design a high-quality ecommerce storefront for a modern consumer brand. Must include at minimum: product discovery, product imagery, product title, price, product detail, variant selection where relevant, primary purchase CTA, trust/support information, responsive mobile purchase experience."

## Hypothesis
**This is a hypothesis, not an observed result.**
Universal UI V2 is expected to perform strongly because the task benefits heavily from:
- product-specific design direction (e.g., immersive image hero, display typography).
- asset planning and visual hierarchy (ensuring the product imagery leads the layout).
- CTA priority and mobile commerce UX mapping.
- The iterative critic loop ensuring information density isn't compromised by generic padding.

## Phase A: Baseline AI
- **Total Score**: 33 / 110
- **Revision Cycles**: 1
- **Observations**: Produced a generic 50/50 layout with basic blue buttons and standard system fonts.

## Phase B: Taste-simulated control
- **Total Score**: 68 / 110
- **Revision Cycles**: 1
- **Observations**: Successfully enforced brutalist, high-contrast layouts. Striking, but a bit too aggressive/developer-focused for a general consumer product.

## Phase C: Universal UI Skills V2 Engine
- **Total Score**: 93 / 110
- **Revision Cycles**: 3
- **Design Dials**: `IMAGE_PROMINENCE=10`, `TYPOGRAPHIC_CHARACTER=6`, `BRAND_EXPRESSIVENESS=7`
- **Observations**: Generated a highly premium, editorial layout utilizing 'Playfair Display', an immersive asset header, and sticky-scroll editorial buying flow.

## Conclusion
- **V2 vs Baseline**: +60 Points
- **V2 vs Taste-simulated**: +25 Points
- The hypothesis was supported: V2 excelled by applying product-specific design direction (editorial luxury) rather than reverting to either standard AI slop or rigid brutalism.
