# Benchmark Scoring Rubric

Do not evaluate benchmarks merely on "looks better." Use this strict 110-point scoring matrix.

| Dimension | Description | Max Score |
|---|---|---|
| **Design Quality** | Overall aesthetic polish, contrast, and visual harmony. | 10 |
| **Visual Hierarchy** | Is the primary CTA obvious? Is data scannable? | 10 |
| **Typography** | Effective use of scale, weight, tracking, and font pairing. | 10 |
| **Composition** | Structural layout choices (e.g., Bento, Asymmetric) vs generic centering. | 10 |
| **Distinctiveness** | Does it feel bespoke, or like a generic template? | 10 |
| **Product Fit** | Does the emotional/visual tone match the target audience? | 10 |
| **Responsive Quality** | Does it reflow intelligently across breakpoints? | 10 |
| **Accessibility** | Contrast ratios, touch targets, and semantics. | 10 |
| **Motion** | Are animations purposeful and performant? | 10 |
| **Consistency** | Adherence to a unified design token system. | 10 |
| **Anti-slop** | Absence of lazy, repetitive AI-generated design tropes. | 10 |

## Total Score: /110
