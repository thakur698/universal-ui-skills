# Unseen Task 03: Custom Haute Horlogerie Configurator

## Status: PENDING REPRODUCTION

## Task Brief (External / Out-of-Distribution)
"Design an ultra-luxury bespoke mechanical watch configurator. Users customize dial guilloché patterns, case metallurgy (rose gold, platinum, forged carbon), hand styles, and strap leathers with real-time bill-of-materials pricing updates, high-resolution material close-ups, and a formal provenance certificate preview."

---

## Required Evidence Contract
1. **Material Fidelity**: Visual depth showcasing textural differences between brushed platinum, gold, and carbon.
2. **Interactive State Synchronization**: Selecting an option instantly updates the live composite view, price, and spec breakdown.
3. **Restrained Luxury Aesthetics**: Editorial typography, micro-borders, and disciplined color palette (no generic neon glow).
4. **Provenance Preview**: Modal or split pane displaying bespoke serial allocation and craft certificate.
5. **Mobile Viewport Fit**: Clean adaptation of complex side-by-side customization panels to vertical screen space.
