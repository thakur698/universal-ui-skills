## Task Brief (External / Out-of-Distribution)
"Design an immersive, editorial scroll-storytelling experience exploring the Hadal zone (Mariana Trench at 11,000m depth). As the user scrolls deeper, ambient pressure telemetry increases, lighting attenuates into total bioluminescent darkness, acoustic bathymetry maps reveal benthic topography, and un-crewed submersible discoveries are progressively disclosed."

---

## Required Evidence Contract
1. **Depth-Synchronized Progression**: Scroll offset directly maps to depth meter (0m -> 11,000m) and atmospheric pressure calculations.
2. **Atmospheric Lighting Shift**: Continuous background gradient transition from photic zone blue to abyssal pitch black.
3. **Pinned Bathymetric Storytelling**: Pinned submersible telemetry panel with synchronized benthic fauna reveals.
4. **Bioluminescent Highlighting**: Controlled luminous accents that preserve WCAG AA text legibility against dark oceanic depths.
5. **Reduced-Motion Fallback**: `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` offers a clean, non-pinned vertical editorial chapter index.
