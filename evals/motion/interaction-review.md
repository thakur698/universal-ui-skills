# Motion & Interaction Evaluation

## Purpose
- [ ] Does the animation clearly explain a state change or reinforce hierarchy?
- [ ] Does the motion focus the user's attention on the correct element?

## Ergonomics
- [ ] Is the animation duration short enough (typically 150-300ms) so it doesn't delay interaction?
- [ ] Is the easing curve natural (e.g., ease-out for entering, ease-in for exiting)?
- [ ] Is perpetual decorative movement avoided unless strictly required for the brand?

## Accessibility
- [ ] Is the animation disabled or significantly reduced when `prefers-reduced-motion` is true?
